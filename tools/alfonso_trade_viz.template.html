Zone lines are recovered from each trade’s entry and padded stop rather than from the logged setup text, which rounds to two decimals and is unusable on FX. Candles are aggregated from the 1‑minute cache to roughly 240 bars per window, so the interval differs per trade and is stated on each chart. Trade timestamps in the run output are bar close times, so the in/out/best markers are drawn on the bar that ends at the stamp — placing them by the stamp itself puts them one candle late, on a bar whose range need not contain the fill price.
